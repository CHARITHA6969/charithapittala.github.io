ENGINEERING APPROACH

Make it useful.
Make it reliable.

Start with the problem. Understand users, workflows, constraints, and success criteria before choosing tools.

Build in verifiable steps. Prefer small increments, explicit interfaces, tests, and observable behaviour.

Respect evidence. Distinguish measured results from assumptions, and source-grounded facts from model output.

Design for operation. Consider security, failure modes, latency, privacy, and maintainability from the beginning.
